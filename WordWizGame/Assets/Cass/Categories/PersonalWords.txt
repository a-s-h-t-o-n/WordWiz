-FIRST NAME-
Personal name given to someone at birth. It's used before a family/last name. E.g Sarah, Melissa.

-MARRIAGE-
Legal union of two people as romantic partners.

-HOUSE-
A building that humans live in.

-APARTMENT-
A set of rooms in a larger building that humans live in.

-PHONE NUMBER-
A number assigned to a phone, used to communicate with others.

-WORK-
A paid activity involving physical or mental effort to achieve a result. 

-AGE-
Length of time a person has lived in years.

-LAST NAME-
A person's family name, passed down from parents to children. Also referred to as a surname.

-SHOPPING LIST-
A list of purchases to be made.
***

-PRODUCE-
Fresh fruits and vegetables.
***

-DAIRY-
Milk, cheese, yogurt and other products made from milk.
***

-MEATS-
Beef, chicken, pork and seafood.
***

-BAKERY-
Fresh bread, pastries and cakes.
***

-FROZEN FOODS-
Frozen meals, vegetables and desserts.
***

-BEST BY-
The date the item should be used before for its best quality.
***

-CASH REGISTER-
Where you pay for your items.
***

-SELF-CHECKOUT-
Area where you can scan and pay for your own items.
***

-RECEIPT-
Printed purchase statement that lists all items bought, their cost and the total amount paid.
***

-AISLE-
A passage between rows of shelves where products are displayed.
***

-EGGS-
An animal product laid by birds, often sold by the dozen. Often described by dozen e.g a dozen eggs.
***

-CASHIER-
Store worker that scans items and processes customer payments.
***

-BAGGING AREA-
Store section near checkout where items are placed into bags after purchase.
***

-CREDIT CARD-
A plastic card used to make payments.
***

-BAG-
Flexible container usually made of plastic, paper or fabric used for carrying things.
***

-MILK-
A dairy product. Often described by bottle, jar or carton e.g a bottle of milk.
***

-APPLE-
A fruit, typically red or green.
***

-CHEESE-
A dairy product made from milk. Often described by packet or bag e.g a packet of cheese.
***

-SALE-
A period during which products are sold at reduced costs.
***

-USE BY-
The date the item should be used by.
***
